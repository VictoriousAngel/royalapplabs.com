Royal Coins

Catalog, identify, and value your coin collection.

Photograph a coin and let AI name it, suggest a grade, and show you why. Then see what your whole collection is worth, and leaf through it like a fine album.

Coming in 2027 iPhone & iPad In development

Inside Royal Coins

Everything your collection needs, in one place.

  • Photograph Each Coin

    A guided camera takes the front, back, and edge, then trims and straightens each photo around the coin.

  • Identify And Grade With AI

    Claude, the AI from Anthropic, names the coin, suggests a grade, and shows its reasons on your own photos. You decide what to keep.

  • Know What It's Worth

    Values from published price guides, with each coin's metal worth at today's prices as a floor.

  • The Coin Book

    Leaf through your collection like a fine album, page by page, coin by coin.

  • Insights

    See what your collection holds and how its value changes over time.

  • Yours, In Your iCloud

    No account to make. Your collection syncs between your iPhone and iPad through your own iCloud, and exports to CSV, text, or PDF whenever you like.

Private By Design

Your collection stays yours.

We built Royal Coins so we never need to see what you collect.

Read the Royal Coins privacy policy →

  • Your collection lives on your devices and in your own iCloud. We can't see it.
  • Coin photos leave your device only when you ask the AI to identify a coin, and are deleted right after.
  • No tracking, and we never sell your information or share it with advertisers.
