Rosemary

An API for Sage 50.

Rosemary is our first service for businesses: a way for the other software you rely on to work with Sage 50, built for the businesses that run on it and the developers who support them.

In development Works with Sage 50

What We're Building Toward

Your accounting, better connected.

Sage 50 holds the numbers a business runs on. Rosemary is meant to make that information easier, and safer, for the rest of your software to work with.

  • Connect Your Tools

    Let the other software your business uses work with Sage 50 through a single, consistent API.

  • Built For Developers

    A clear, well-documented API that's straightforward to build on, whether you're in-house or a consultant.

  • Careful With Financial Data

    Accounting data deserves care. Security and privacy are part of Rosemary's design from the start.
